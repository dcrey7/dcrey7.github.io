What the site collects

  • Visiting the site. Cloudflare, the host, receives your IP address, browser type and the pages you ask for, so it can deliver the site and block attacks. Google may then set cookies or similar identifiers under its own privacy policy. Legal basis: your consent, given by pressing play (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). To avoid it, do not press play.
  • The review form. The form on the PEOPLE screen is switched off at the moment and collects nothing. When it is on, it sends your name, title and company, how we worked together, the year, a contact (LinkedIn or email), your text and an optional photo to Supabase, Inc. (servers in the EU). Legal basis: your consent. Pending entries that I do not approve are deleted within 6 months. Approved entries stay on the site until you ask me to remove them. Your contact detail is never shown on the site.
  • Email. If you write to me, I keep the email in my mailbox (Gmail, Google) as long as I need it to answer, and at most 3 years after our last exchange. Legal basis: legitimate interest in answering you.
  • Settings on your device. The site saves four settings in your browser's local storage: xmb-sound (sound on or off), xmb-theme (background theme), xmb-mode (dark or light) and xmb-lang (language). They stay on your device, are never sent to anyone, and you can clear them in your browser settings. They only remember the choices you make, so they need no consent (French law, art. 82 of the Loi Informatique et Libertés).

  • I do not sell or share your data, and I do not use it to train AI models.
