Privacy policy for youtube-dcrey7-stats

youtube-dcrey7-stats is a private tool. Only the owner of the dcrey7 YouTube channel uses it.

It reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics numbers with read-only access. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything.

The numbers stay on the owner's computer. The tool does not share, sell or send them to anyone.

The tool collects no data about other people.

To remove access, open your Google Account, then Security, then Third-party apps, and remove the app.
